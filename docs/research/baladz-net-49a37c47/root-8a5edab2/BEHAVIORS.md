# Behaviors

## Interaction model

- Navigation: click-driven client state with a soft opacity transition.
- News lead: previous/next buttons and dot selection update the lead item.
- Floating promotion: close button hides it for the current session.
- Links/icons: subtle opacity or background hover transition, 160-200ms.
- No evidence of scroll-driven animation, smooth-scroll library, modal, or dark mode in supplied captures.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop 1440-1920: centered content with a fixed promotion at the right edge.
- Tablet: content narrows and poster aligns beside/under primary content.
- Mobile 390: single column, horizontal tab strip, compact logo and icon rows, poster rendered inline.
